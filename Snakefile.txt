# --- Pipeline steps --- #
## peak_analysis: Peak analysis using DANPOS3 (Step 8)

## read_count:Estimate mean nuclear genome cover with Picard (Step 7b)

## size_metrics: Calculate alignment statistics with Picard (Step 7a)

## correct_GC_bias: Correct GC bias with deeptools (Step 6c)

## compute_GC_bias: Compute GC bias with deeptools (Step 6b)

## effective_genome_size: Calculate effetcive genome size with faCount (Step 6a)

## alignment_deduplication: Remove PCR and optial duplicates from alignment with Picard (Step 5)

## read_alignment: Align reads with kalign (Step 4)

## extract_UMI: UMI extraction with FGBio and Picard (Step 3)

## raw_read_QC: Perform quality control using FastQC (Step 2)

## suffix_array: Create suffix array with kalign (Step 1b)

## mask_repeats: Masking with RepeatMasker (Step 1a)
